JazzSAMBA

A multitrack dataset of jazz standards recorded both asynchronously (per-instrument sessions) and synchronously (full band together), with timed bar, chord, section, and soloist annotations for live music models.

Why JazzSAMBA

Interactive accompaniment and multitrack MIR need realistic combo audio with reliable structure labels. JazzSAMBA pairs original studio performances with a shared bar grid, better/worse take tiers, and public stem FLACs on Zenodo.

  • Dual recording protocols on the same repertoire (async vs sync).
  • Per-song better/worse take pairs at mixture and stem level.
  • Multi-layer annotations (bars, chords, sections, soloists) on a bar grid.
  • Ground-truth MIDI for piano (and drums on async); derived AMT/debleed marked clearly.

Featured mixes

Better-take stereo MP3s for the site (24 kHz / 192 kbps). Full stems and worse takes ship in the dataset download. Two asynchronous and two synchronous slots — song picks TBD.
