The Band

Eight musicians recorded JazzSAMBA across asynchronous and synchronous sessions. Most have played together since high school; song rosters vary by track, including which bassist and saxophonist plays.

Hear featured clips on the JazzSAMBA home page, browse full takes in Explore (filter by musician, genre, form, and more), and find the dataset download, Python package, and processing code from there.
